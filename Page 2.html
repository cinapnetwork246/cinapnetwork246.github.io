Meet Our Overseer

APOSTLE KERRY W. MASON

Apostle Kerry Wendell Mason was born and raised in the beautiful island of Barbados in the Eastern Caribbean. He gave his heart to the Lord at age 15 at a revival crusade at a church in his home community. He was filled with the Holy Spirit in 2002 and began preaching the Word of God one year later.
Mason was ordained as a minister in 2004 and has served in five churches under five pastors between 2004 and 2023 in the capacities of musician, singer, worship leader, sound engineer, music producer and choir director. He began flowing in the prophetic and preaching ministry during this time.
In 2024, Mason was ordained and set apart as a prophet of God. During this time, he has traveled to three nations preaching and prophesying, and operating in the healing and deliverance ministry. In 2025, he was confirmed as an apostle.
In January 2026, Mason founded Kings & Priests International Ministries. The ministry started online and it quickly gained traction in his home land of Barbados as well as overseas with average attendance of thirty person per session with attendees in Barbados and all over the world.

Apostle Mason flows with strong prophetic accuracy with signs and wonders following. He also is a powerful preacher and teacher of the Word of God.
